Convergence criteria

NonconvexCore provides convergence criteria that solver packages can use to check the quality of a solution against a model.

A Workspace collects the workspace required to evaluate a criterion, and the criteria themselves are:

  • GenericCriteria: convergence based on the change in the solution and the objective, and the constraint violation.
  • KKTCriteria and ScaledKKTCriteria: convergence based on the KKT residuals and the constraint violation.
  • IpoptCriteria: convergence based on Ipopt's optimality error and the constraint violation.

The thresholds of a criterion are configured with Tolerance.
